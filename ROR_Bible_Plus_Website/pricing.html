Questions

Plans and access

Which features need a subscription?

Some features and resources require a subscription. Available editions, audio, resources and functions depend on your plan, content catalogue and supported device.

Can I use one account on all my devices?

Yes. With your ROR Bible+ account, your notes, highlights and bookmarks sync across supported devices.

Do I need an internet connection?

Online services require an internet connection. Bible Lite, the progressive web app, offers offline access to your Bible study.

How do I get help?

Contact support from inside the ROR Bible+ app, or reach us through the Support page.
